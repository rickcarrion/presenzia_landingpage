Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know about technology?

No. We build the website, maintain it and set up the measurement. You just tell us about your work and read your report each month.

How do you know how many contacts became clients?

The website records every person who writes to you through WhatsApp or the form. Once a month we ask you how many of them hired you and add it to your report.

Why is there a 6-month minimum?

Because showing up on Google is not immediate: it usually takes 3 to 6 months. That time lets us show you real results instead of promises.

Do you guarantee a number of clients?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. What we do guarantee is that every month you'll know exactly what your website generated, so you can decide where to invest with data.

What does the monthly fee cover?

The domain, hosting, maintenance of your website, the work to get you found on Google and your monthly report.
